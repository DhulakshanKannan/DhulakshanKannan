04 — Research

Featured Research

Daily-Based PM2.5 Pollution Pattern Identification using Fuzzy C-Means Clustering: A Case Study of the Colombo Municipal Council Area

HND Research Project · National Institute of Business Management (NIBM), in collaboration with Coventry University
  • Built a probabilistic preprocessing framework (Normal, Lognormal, Gamma, Weibull fitting with AIC-based model selection) on 3 years of PM2.5 data from 12 monitoring stations.
  • Applied Min-Max normalization and Fuzzy C-Means clustering, validated with FCSETS-based stability checks.
  • Identified 5 distinct daily pollution patterns, revealing weekday–weekend and station-level trends to support air quality management.
